# Our Story

Still Point Yoga began as a humble community effort to create a peaceful place for yoga and meditation. Over the years, it has blossomed into a vibrant studio that continues to uphold its core values of mindfulness, respect, and balance.

# Our Mission

We aim to cultivate a welcoming environment where practitioners of all levels can grow, heal, and reconnect with themselves.
